Manage alerts

An alert has two independent parts, both set up here once you've got an account:

  • A channel — the email address or Discord webhook alerts actually get delivered to. Add one first; without it, nothing has anywhere to go.
  • An alert area — where to watch. Either a point + radius (drawn from the form or by clicking the map), or a whole city, chosen by name. Point areas can auto-expire once the spot stops producing detections, or stay durable until you cancel them; city alerts always stay until cancelled.

Every channel you register gets every alert from every area you set up — there's no per-area routing. Once both exist, each scheduled reload checks fresh detections against your areas and sends anything that matches.

Log in or create an account to set these up.

Notification channels

Register the email address or Discord webhook your alerts actually get delivered to. This is separate from your alert areas (and the map's click-to-subscribe popups) — those just say where to watch; the channels here say how to reach you. Every channel you add gets every alert from every one of your alert areas and city alerts below.
