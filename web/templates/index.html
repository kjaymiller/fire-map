What is this?

Fire Map pulls active fire detections from NASA's FIRMS VIIRS data and plots them on a map.

Want an alert when a fire shows up near a point you care about? Create an account, then set up a location + radius. Already got one? Manage alerts.

About confidence: each detection is rated by NASA's VIIRS algorithm from least to most certain it actually saw fire (rather than sun glint, hot ground, or another false positive): low < nominal < high. "Nominal" is the normal, most common confidence level, not a step below "low" — most detections fall here. Low-confidence points are the most likely to be noise; high-confidence points are the most likely to be a real, sizable fire.

This is not an emergency tool. Data here is pulled from satellite passes on a delay and does not represent real-time conditions. Do not use this map for evacuation decisions or emergency planning — consult your local fire authority or emergency services for that.
